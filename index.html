How your $20 works

Small gifts, pooled, add up to a seat at the table.

  1. You join

    Members give $20 a year or more. Every 25 memberships fund one $500 contribution to a legislator who champions our students.

  2. Trustees choose champions

    SEA-PAC's Board of Trustees backs legislators who have stood up for special education funding and services.

  3. We report every dollar

    Every contribution is filed with the Illinois State Board of Elections and shared with members and the IAASE Governing Board.

How we decide

We support legislators of either party who champion students with disabilities. SEA-PAC's Board of Trustees votes on every contribution.
