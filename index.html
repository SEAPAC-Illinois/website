How your $20 works

Small gifts, pooled, add up to a seat at the table.

  1. You join

    Members give $20 a year or more. 25 memberships add up to $500, the size of a typical contribution to a legislator who champions our students.

  2. Trustees choose champions

    SEA-PAC's Board of Trustees backs legislators who have stood up for special education funding and services.

  3. We report every dollar

    Every contribution goes on our public quarterly report to the Illinois State Board of Elections once the check is cashed, and into our updates to the IAASE Governing Board.

How we decide

We back legislators who champion students with disabilities, whatever their party. Trustees look at whether a legislator supports the bills IAASE backs and works against the bills IAASE opposes. SEA-PAC's Board of Trustees votes on every contribution. Know a legislator we should support? Email support@seapacil.com.

Questions

Good to know before you join.

Is my membership tax-deductible?

No. SEA-PAC is a political committee, so contributions are not tax-deductible for federal income tax purposes.

I work for a school district. Can I join?

Yes, as a private citizen. Join with your own money, from your personal email and your own device, on your own time. Illinois school districts must have an ethics policy that bars employees from political activity during paid work time or with district property. If you are unsure, check your district's policy.

Can my district give, or can I use a school card or account?

No. SEA-PAC never accepts school district money. Never use a district card, account, or email to give.

Who can join?

Anyone. SEA-PAC accepts contributions from individuals, from their own funds. Organizations, please email support@seapacil.com before giving. We cannot accept anonymous contributions, contributions made in another person's name, or contributions from foreign nationals.

Is SEA-PAC part of IAASE? Do my IAASE dues go to SEA-PAC?

No IAASE money goes to SEA-PAC. SEA-PAC is the political action committee affiliated with IAASE, with its own treasurer and its own reports. Joining is completely optional and has nothing to do with your IAASE membership.

Can I give by check?

Yes. Make the check payable to Special Education Administrators' PAC (SEAPAC), our name on file with the State Board of Elections. Email support@seapacil.com for the mailing address, and include your home address with your check.

I'm already a member. Do I need to do anything?

If you joined with a card, PayPal, Apple Pay, or Google Pay and chose auto-renew, you are set. Your Givebutter receipt has a link to change or cancel. If you paid with Venmo, please rejoin each year, because Venmo payments do not renew on their own. Not sure if you are current, or need to update your address? Email support@seapacil.com and we will check.

Where is my receipt, and how do I change or cancel?

Givebutter emails a receipt as soon as you join. Use the link in it to change or cancel, or email support@seapacil.com and we will do it for you.

Who decides where the money goes?

SEA-PAC's Board of Trustees votes on every contribution. See "How we decide" above.

I'm a legislator or on a legislator's staff. How do I reach SEA-PAC?

SEA-PAC Illinois is the political action committee of Illinois special education leaders, affiliated with IAASE. Our Chair is Jon Malone. To request support or to collaborate, email support@seapacil.com. Our Treasurer, Shannon Miller Bellini, or Melissa Taylor of IAASE will follow up. Illinois State Board of Elections Committee ID: 7464.
