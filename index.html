The PAC of Illinois special education administrators

For $20 a year, you help special education leaders build relationships with the legislators who decide how students with disabilities are served and funded.

SEA-PAC Illinois is affiliated with the Illinois Alliance of Administrators of Special Education (IAASE). Members come from every IAASE region of the state.

Why SEA-PAC

Special education needs a voice in Springfield.

SEA-PAC works to make sure the voices of special education administrators are heard in the legislative process. We back legislators and policies that improve special education for Illinois students.

Build relationships

We support legislators who champion students with disabilities and the professionals who serve them.

Keep members informed

Members get short updates on bills and funding decisions that affect special education.

Answer to members

A Board of Trustees governs SEA-PAC. We report on our activities and finances to the IAASE Governing Board and to members.
