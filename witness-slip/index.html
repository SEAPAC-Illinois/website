How to file a witness slip

A witness slip tells an Illinois House or Senate committee where you stand on a bill before it votes. It takes about two minutes.

  • WhatAn online form that puts your name and position on the record for one committee hearing.
  • WhenOnly after the bill is scheduled for a hearing, and before that hearing starts.
  • What to pickThe position named in our alert, and "Record of Appearance Only."

Step by step

File your slip

  1. Open the button in our alert.

    It goes straight to the slip form for the right bill and hearing. No alert handy? Go to ilga.gov (opens in a new tab), look up the bill number, open its Witness Slip tab, pick the hearing, and click the create slip icon next to the bill.

  2. Fill in your contact details.

    Your name, home address, personal email, and phone. Sign in first if you made an account (see below) and these fill in for you.

  3. Say who you represent.

    Most of us file as private citizens:

    • Firm/Business or Agency: Self
    • Title: NA
    • Representation: Self

  4. Choose your position.

    Pick the one our alert names: Proponent (for the bill) or Opponent (against it). If the alert names an amendment, choose that amendment, not the original bill.

  5. Choose "Record of Appearance Only."

    This records your position without asking you to testify. Skip oral and written testimony unless you plan to speak or send a statement.

  6. Agree to the terms and click "Create Slip."

    Complete the "I'm not a robot" check if asked. You'll get a copy of your slip by email.

Deadline: before the hearing starts

You can file as soon as the hearing is posted. File the day before if you can, so nothing gets missed. Each hearing needs its own slip, so if the bill moves to the other chamber, watch for a new alert.

Optional, but handy

Make a free account before January

You can file without one. An account fills in your details, saves your slips, and lets you fix a slip later. If you had an account on the old General Assembly site, you need a new one.

  1. On ilga.gov (opens in a new tab), click Login at the top right, then Register.

  2. Enter your personal email and a password, agree to the terms, and finish the robot check.

  3. Open the confirmation email, enter the code, and click Confirm Registration.

Avoid these

Common mistakes

  • Listing your school district or employer.Use "Self" unless your organization has asked you to file for it.
  • Choosing oral or written testimony by accident."Record of Appearance Only" is all you need.
  • Filing after the hearing starts.A late slip may not count. Aim for the day before.
  • Picking the wrong bill, amendment, or hearing.Match the bill number and committee to the alert.
  • Picking a different position than the alert.Check it before you click "Create Slip."
  • Assuming one slip covers every hearing.A slip counts for one hearing only.

File as a private citizen

Use your personal email and device, on your own time, never school email or equipment. Slips are public: anyone can see who filed on a bill and their position.

Questions

I made a mistake. Can I fix my slip?

If you were signed in when you filed, you can edit it from your account. If not, file a new slip with the right answers.

How do I know which position to choose?

Every SEA-PAC alert names the position to pick for that bill. This guide covers how to file, not where to stand.
